base/waist interfaces; mounting bridge; assembly alignment; fit coupons;
reproducible CAD generation, checking scripts and documentation.
Unchanged upstream printable geometry is identified by U-prefixed file names.
Those delivery STL files receive rigid transforms only for print orientation.
M-prefixed files are modified upstream designs; N-prefixed files are new adapters.

Servo and metal-horn reference envelopes are approximate mounting envelopes,
not manufacturer CAD or a certification of compatibility. R1 requires physical
fit and load testing before use. No payload rating or print-ready G-code is supplied.

R1: drive gear axial offset corrected by +1.5 mm; jaw axial alignment and spacer instructions corrected. Original tooth profiles retained. Geometric chain backlash remains approximately 8.2-11.6 degrees in the assessed range.
